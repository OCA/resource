Label your resources with what they can do.

Every ``resource.resource`` — a person, a vehicle, a room, a machine —
can be tagged with the **roles** it is able to fulfil: Technician,
Supervisor, Driver, Hair Stylist… whatever fits your business. This is a
plain capability label, independent of HR job positions.

**Tags** offer a second, free-form classification for anything else you
need to track — certifications, regions, equipment classes.

Roles and tags show up on the resource form, can be used to group and
filter the resource list, and are the vocabulary other planning modules
(like ``resource_planning``) use to say who or what may cover a job.

Usage
=====

under the planning Configuration menus.
